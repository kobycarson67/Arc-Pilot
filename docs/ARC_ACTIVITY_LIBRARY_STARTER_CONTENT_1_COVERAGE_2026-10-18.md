## Differentiation

WT starters emphasize coached setup, foundational measurement/layout, introductory cutting or SMAW control, and required instructor checkpoints. AWT starters require prior WT preparation, greater process independence, drawing-driven planning, diagnosis, controlled parameter decisions, inspection, and corrective action.

The differentiation is encoded in separate prerequisite, technical-complexity, expected-independence, assessment, and readiness fields. WT and AWT items are separate records rather than text variants.
