# ARC CLASSROOM READINESS CONTRACT

**Purpose:** A finite finish line for putting real Students into ARC and using the classroom-facing system as intended. This is separate from both the v7→v8 parity contract and the long-range roadmap.

## Rule
A feature is not Classroom Ready merely because its service/schema exists. It must have the required authority, usable UI, necessary starter content, recovery/privacy protections, cross-view consistency, and physical Samsung acceptance appropriate to that feature.

Resources and Help may follow Classroom Ready. Long-range roadmap items do not block Classroom Ready unless explicitly promoted later.

## Administration minimum
### Weekly Lesson Plans
Generate the **upcoming week** on Friday for instructor review/edit/export. Use authoritative calendar, Sections, pacing, Curriculum, Standards/Essentials, approved Lessons, targets, success criteria, differentiation, engagement and assessment. Administration-accepted WT/AWT examples define the desired output family; they are examples, not district policy.

### SLO Documentation
On-demand Data Cycle/SLO package derived from authentic ARC Evidence/history. Prepopulate facts only. Instructor reflection/rationale, IEP-specific decisions, reteach/extend decisions, administrator feedback/approval and signatures remain human authority.

### Reports
At minimum preserve the recovered intent for administrator-ready curriculum/Standards coverage, Essential Standards evidence, Student growth/evidence and other supported class/program reports. Exact report catalog should be explicitly frozen before implementation.

### Generated Documents / Exports
A home for ARC-generated administrative artifacts rather than forcing the instructor to rebuild information manually.

## Activity Library launch requirement
Do not wait for an exhaustive library. Do not launch it empty.

Seed enough WT/AWT content to support current classroom operation across:
- Projects;
- Technical Assignments;
- Skill Challenges;
- Practice.

Starter content should cover the common pathways represented by the authoritative Curriculum/Standards and buy time for continued expansion after launch. Exact initial counts/content require a bounded curation stage, not arbitrary filler.

## Non-sidebar Classroom Ready gates
- authoritative v8 cutover without mixed v7/v8 truth;
- backup/export/restore integrity;
- verified local durability;
- approved OneDrive sync/recovery or an explicitly accepted temporary recovery boundary before real Student data;
- FERPA/privacy review appropriate to real Student data;
- photo/artifact protection;
- safe migrations;
- no destructive reset path against protected production;
- Samsung direct-Chrome and installed-PWA verification;
- offline/reload/update/rotation/background/sleep-wake behavior;
- clear rollback/recovery procedure;
- no fictional/test data in classroom production.

## Not blockers for first Classroom Ready release
Resources, Help, ARC Student, PIN login, AI Weld Coach, cleanup timer, Student inspection requests, Project Planner, 2D/3D authoring, Community Project Bank, Student Showcase/portfolio, predictive purchasing, advanced cut optimization, other CTE pathways and commercial multi-school architecture.

These remain preserved roadmap authority and must not be deleted merely because they are not launch blockers.
