# Administration Generator Results

Date: 2026-10-18
Status: local review surface

The Administration sidebar action generates three editable JSON review drafts:

1. Upcoming-week plans use the current local school date, authoritative calendar output, current Sections, and recorded pacing facts. A missing Lesson remains `not-scheduled`; PD, no-school, and `none` instruction remain visible.
2. SLO documentation summarizes only recorded Evidence and history. Rationale, reflection, IEP decisions, reteach or extend decisions, administrator feedback, approval, and signatures remain `null` for human completion.
3. The readiness report contains Lesson and curriculum coverage, competency-catalog and recorded-rating counts, Activity coverage, current grade snapshots produced by the existing grade owner, growth facts, and P4–P10C authority status.

All three require explicit export from an editable text area. Nothing is submitted, signed, approved, graded, accepted, or written to an external system automatically.
