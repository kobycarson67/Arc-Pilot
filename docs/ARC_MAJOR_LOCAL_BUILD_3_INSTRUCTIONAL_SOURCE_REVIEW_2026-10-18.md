## Course differentiation controls

- WT preserves the 13207 course-description requirement for **SMAW and thermal cutting**, including lap, T, corner, edge, and butt joint opportunities and bounded single-bevel butt certification-practice plates, inspection, and bend-test practice without claiming certification.
- AWT requires WT preparation and centers GMAW, independent setup/diagnosis, fabrication planning, inspection/correction, career readiness, and only an **instructor-selected** additional advanced process.
- Every record exposes the exact preserved target, criterion, strategies, and assessment plus a lesson-specific model, guided application, readiness check, Student options, targeted feedback, and possible next work.
- Reusable and numbered Lessons remain separate; the review does not force all Students into one weekly sequence.
