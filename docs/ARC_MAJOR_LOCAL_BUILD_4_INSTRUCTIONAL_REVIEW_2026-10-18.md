## Whole-unit review model

Each source-grounded unit is presented as:

1. **Introduce:** establish exact concepts, vocabulary, safety boundaries, and observable criteria.
2. **Guided application:** instructor demonstration and supervised Student work with explicit hold points.
3. **Independent or extension:** readiness-based application with course-appropriate independence.
4. **Inspect and decide:** Student self-check, instructor observation, specific feedback, and an explicit next-path decision.

The review surface displays up to three representative Lessons per unit in full teaching-detail form. It also displays only candidate Activity relevance and carries unresolved mappings visibly. It cannot accept content, assign work, schedule a Lesson, create Evidence, post a grade, qualify a competency, or unlock an Activity.

## Course differentiation

WT retains foundational measurement, layout, cutting, SMAW, joint preparation, fit-up, squareness, safety, inspection, and correction. Students receive modeling and bounded choices; the instructor decides readiness for independent work.

AWT requires prior WT preparation and adds drawing interpretation, fabrication planning, GMAW setup and troubleshooting, dimensional control, independent process decisions, discontinuity diagnosis, corrective planning, inspection, and career-quality documentation. The additional advanced process remains instructor-selected.

## Activity relationships

Skill Challenges remain bounded demonstrations. Practice remains non-graded and nonqualifying by default. Every relationship is `CANDIDATE_RELEVANCE_ONLY` and awaits instructor review.
