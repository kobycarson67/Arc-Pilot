## Scope

This bounded rehearsal extends the accepted end-to-end academic activation rehearsal with the accepted Semester 1 six-section topology. It uses the actual normal `index.html`, actual production-path academic owners and activation coordinator, real IndexedDB, the service worker, and installed Microsoft Edge in an exclusively task-owned profile. It does not use Samsung bytes, protected production data, or real Student data.
