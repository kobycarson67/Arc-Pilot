# Lesson Plan Review Rubric Faithful Transcription

Source photographs: `../source_material/450.jpg` and `../source_material/451.jpg`.

Administrative fields: Month of; Administrator; Date Reviewed; Lesson Reflections completed and submitted on; Overall Lesson Plan Proficiency Level; Notes/Comments.

Rating columns: Advanced (4), Proficient (3), Basic (2), Below Basic (1).

## Essential Standards

- **Advanced (4):** Priority/essential standards are clearly identified and represent the critical learning for the unit. Standards are appropriately sequenced and aligned to learning targets.
- **Proficient (3):** Essential standards are identified and represent the critical learning of the unit and are aligned to learning targets but are not sequenced appropriately or lack clarity.
- **Basic (2):** Essential standards are identified but do not represent the critical learning and/or are not aligned to learning targets appropriately sequenced.
- **Below Basic (1):** Essential Standards are missing, inaccurate, or not aligned to instruction.

## Learning Targets

- **Advanced (4):** Daily/Weekly learning targets are written in student-friendly language, are clear and concise, measurable, and aligned to essential standards.
- **Proficient (3):** Learning targets align to standards and are measurable but are not written in clear student friendly language.
- **Basic (2):** Learning targets are partially aligned to standards but are not measurable and are not written clearly.
- **Below Basic (1):** Learning Targets are missing or are not aligned to identified standards.

## Success Criteria

- **Advanced (4):** Success criteria are clearly defined, measurable and aligned to learning targets. Ensuring students know exactly what is expected.
- **Proficient (3):** Success criteria align with learning targets and clearly define what the student is expected to do but are not measurable.
- **Basic (2):** Success criteria are stated but do not align to learning targets and are not clear on what students are expected to do.
- **Below Basic (1):** Success criteria are not aligned to learning targets and/or are missing.

## Engagement Strategies

- **Advanced (4):** Multiple, purposeful, engagement strategies to actively involve all students throughout the lesson are identified and aligned to the learning target with the purpose of promoting ownership, allowing movement, giving choice, and focusing on higher-level thinking and project-based learning.
- **Proficient (3):** Appropriate engagement strategies that provide students with opportunities to actively participate and take ownership of their learning are identified and aligned to the learning targets.
- **Basic (2):** Limited engagement strategies or strategies that rely primarily on passive participation are identified. Opportunities for active student involvement are inconsistent or loosely connected to the learning target.
- **Below Basic (1):** No identifiable engagement strategies are identified. Instruction is primarily teacher-driven with minimal opportunity for active student participation. Engagement strategies are simply a list of assignments.

## Assessment

- **Advanced (4):** Multiple planned checks for understanding throughout the lesson are planned and aligned to the learning targets and success criteria. Evidence of interim assessments and vocabulary assessments analysis.
- **Proficient (3):** Planned checks for understanding are planned and aligned to the learning targets but found only at the end of the lesson. Evidence of interim assessments and vocabulary assessments analysis.
- **Basic (2):** Planned checks for understanding include only summative assessments; after the learning is over but are aligned to learning targets. No evidence of interim and vocabulary assessments.
- **Below Basic (1):** No formative or summative assessments are identified or aligned to learning targets.

## Lesson Plan Submission

- **Advanced (4):** Plans are submitted on time by the morning of the first weekday of school; either in PlanBook or an agreed upon format.
- **Proficient (3):** Plans are submitted weekly but do not meet the first weekday of school deadline.
- **Basic (2):** Plans are occasionally submitted sometime during the week; but not on a consistent basis.
- **Below Basic (1):** Plans are seldom submitted weekly and need to be tracked down for evaluation purposes.
