## ARC authority boundary

SLO Documentation is a required on-demand Administrative generator. ARC may prepopulate only facts supported by authentic ARC records. Instructor rationale and reflection, IEP-specific decisions, reteaching/extension decisions, administrator feedback and approval, and signatures require their actual human authorities. SLO output reuses ARC Evidence and history; it does not create a parallel evidence system.
